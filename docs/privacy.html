Privacy Policy

Last updated September 3, 2026

This policy explains what OneBrowse collects and what it does with it. The short version: OneBrowse has no servers and no analytics. What it stores stays on your device, or in your own iCloud if you sign in.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it — OneBrowse has no backend. The app stores the following on your device only:

  • The sites you pin: the name, address, icon, colour, blocking level, Site Lock setting and any background image you choose.
  • Your favourites, your history in Free Browse (private tabs are never recorded), and the tabs that were open when you last used the app.
  • A count of how many pop-ups have been blocked on each site, and the page elements you have chosen to hide.
  • Your settings.

Background images you add are copied into the app's private storage on your device.

iCloud sync

If you sign in with Apple, your pinned sites, favourites, history and settings are mirrored through your own iCloud account so they follow you between your devices. That data travels between your devices and Apple's iCloud under Apple's privacy policy; we never see it and have no server that could. Signing out from Settings stops the mirroring, and Delete Account removes what was mirrored.

Web browsing

When you open a pinned site or search in Free Browse, your device connects directly to that website, exactly as any browser would. Those sites receive what they normally receive from a browser. OneBrowse removes common tracking parameters from links before loading them and upgrades known sites to HTTPS, but it cannot control what a website does with the connection you make.

Search

Free Browse sends what you type to whichever search engine you choose — Google by default, with DuckDuckGo, Bing, Ecosia and Startpage also offered, changeable any time in Settings. That engine handles your search under its own privacy policy. With Search Suggestions on, the letters you type are also sent to that engine as you type so it can offer completions; turn it off in Settings and nothing is sent until you press Go. We do not log, store, or transmit your searches.

Blocking lists and site icons

To block ads and pop-ups OneBrowse downloads published filter lists — EasyList and EasyPrivacy from easylist.to, uBlock Origin's lists from GitHub, Fanboy's lists from fanboy.co.nz, and Peter Lowe's ad-server list from pgl.yoyo.org — about once a week, with nothing about you attached beyond the ordinary web request. To show a pinned site's own icon on its button, OneBrowse fetches that icon from the site once. Copies of the lists ship inside the app so it works before the first download.

What we do NOT do

No advertising. No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. No tracking across apps or websites. No selling or sharing of personal information — there is nothing collected to sell. No location access. No contacts, microphone or camera access. Photo access is used only when you pick a background image, and only the image you choose is read.

Third parties

OneBrowse embeds no third-party SDKs. The external connections it makes are the websites you visit, your chosen search engine, the publishers of the filter lists named above, and Apple's iCloud if you sign in. Apple may collect standard App Store and crash information under its own policies, which we do not control.

Children

OneBrowse is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone. Because it can reach the open web, a parent or guardian should decide whether it is appropriate.

Your control and your rights

Because everything lives on your device or in your own iCloud, you are in direct control. Removing a pinned site deletes its data and its background image. Clear Website Data erases cookies, caches and site storage. Clear History and the Hidden Elements list are in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and the date above will change.
